1. Educational Self-Check Tool (Not an Anti-Plagiarism Service)

VidyaSetu compares your draft only against the reference text you paste into the form. It is an educational self-review aid for students. It does not search the internet, academic databases, or any plagiarism corpus, and a low score is not a guarantee that a submission is free of plagiarism. Always verify against your original sources and your institution's rules.

2. Academic Honesty

  • Use VidyaSetu to review your own work. Submitting another person's writing as your own, or bypassing your institution's official similarity checks, is prohibited.
  • Citation formats generated by this tool are guidance; style requirements vary between schools, journals, and teachers. Confirm the final reference list against your required style guide.
  • Declare AI assistant usage wherever your class, competition, or publisher requires disclosure.

3. Assignment Planner

Effort estimates are simple heuristic calculations based on word count, work type, and daily hours. They are planning suggestions only and are not commitments from any school or instructor. Deadlines you enter are your own responsibility to confirm.
