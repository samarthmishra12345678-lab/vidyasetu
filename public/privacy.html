Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

1. Data Minimization & Zero Account Wall

VidyaSetu operates without user registration, passwords, advertising trackers, or third-party analytics scripts. You do not need to submit your name, phone number, email address, or school identifier to check a draft or build a citation.

2. How Draft and Source Text Is Processed

  • When you click Check Originality, the draft and reference text you paste are sent over HTTPS to POST /api/check solely to compute similarity scores and flagged sentences in memory.
  • VidyaSetu does not write submitted drafts, source passages, or citation fields to any persistent database, log file, or external third-party service.
  • No draft text ever leaves the server memory except back to your own browser tab as the report.
  • Avoid pasting private personal data, unpublished exam content, or another person's private work into the text boxes.

3. Citation, Planner, and Local Browser Storage

  • Citation fields are processed in memory by POST /api/cite and are never stored server-side.
  • Your assignment checklist is stored strictly inside your own browser under the local key vidyasetu_plan_tasks.
  • Your saved reference list stays in your browser under the local key vidyasetu_refs.
  • Audio read-aloud uses your browser's built-in window.speechSynthesis engine locally on your device.

4. Study Exchange Board Entries

Entries submitted to POST /api/board are visible to other visitors during active server sessions and are sanitized to strip HTML tags. Do not post private phone numbers, addresses, or anyone else's unpublished work on public entries. Board memory resets whenever the server restarts, so treat the board as a session notice board rather than a permanent archive.

5. Contact & Data Inquiries

For questions regarding privacy practices or repository maintenance, contact the project maintainer via the official VidyaSetu repository issue tracker.
